Purchase delays

Days between the order landing in Zoho (Order Creation Date) and the Purchase Date. Green = purchased the same or next day, amber = 2–3 days, red = 4 days or more. Percentages are shares of all purchased order lines.

Never purchased — cancelled / refunded, by Zoho lead status

Refunds

Taken from the Zoho Refund column (Full Refund / Partial Refund) against all order lines. “Seller refund received” means the line has a US Seller Refund Date or a Refunded-by-US-Seller amount greater than 0 — either one is enough, even if the other of the two is blank or zero, so a line isn't missed just because one field wasn't filled in.

Refunded lines by Zoho lead status

Refunded, but product not received back

Refunded lines (Full / Partial) whose Zoho lead status says delivered, RMA authorised or Amazon Refunded - Product Not Received. For these the Reverse Pickup Token ID column is checked: if there is no reverse pickup update (and no RTO/RMA received date) the customer has been refunded but the product has not come back.

By Zoho lead status

Pending refunds

A line is a pending refund when the customer's refund is owed but not recorded: its Zoho lead status is on your list (customer cancellation, order failed / cancelled, returned to inventory, refunded-by-seller and similar) or it is another cancelled, failed or returned status (RMA / RTO, FBA, lost, damaged, wrong or not-received item), and the Refund column is still empty. These lines are left out of Total shipments. Lines that were never purchased are counted in "Never purchased — cancelled / refunded" above instead of here, so a line is never counted twice.

Pending refunds by Zoho lead status

DG products — EDD brief

Order lines whose Zoho lead status is a DG (dangerous goods) hold, e.g. Item Held at Delhi - DG Product, and that are not delivered or refunded. Each is compared with the report as-of date: EDD breached = EDD is before the as-of date; otherwise it shows how many days are left before the EDD breaches.

EDD date wise

Shipments — delivered and pending

Shipments = order lines that are not refunded and have no pending refund (nothing owed back to the customer). Delivered on time = delivered on or before the EDD, delivered with delay = delivered after the EDD. On-time and delay percentages are shares of delivered shipments; delivered and pending are shares of all shipments.

Pending to deliver — shipped and not shipped

The Pending to deliver shipments from the Shipments section, split using the Zoho shipping dates (not the lead status). Shipped = the line has a US Shipping Date (shipped from India) or any later shipping date. Not shipped = none of those dates exists yet. Each group is then broken down by how far its shipping dates have got, and by EDD against the report as-of date: breached = EDD is before the as-of date, otherwise the days left before it breaches.

Download full report

Every row exactly as you uploaded it — all original columns, in the original order — with two columns added at the end. Remarks 1 gives every single row the dashboard header it belongs to: Never purchased — cancelled / refunded, Refunded, Refunded product not received back, Pending refunds, DG products — EDD brief, Pending to deliver, Delivered, or Duplicate lead. Remarks 2 gives the sub-grouping shown under that header: Shipped / Not shipped (Pending to deliver), the EDD position (DG products), Delivered on time / with delay, No reverse pickup update, or the Zoho lead status for the refund and never-purchased headers. A line gets exactly one header, checked in that order. This always covers the whole uploaded file, not just what the filters above are currently showing.
